Privacy & Terms

NoScript Ludo · Last updated: 13 October 2026

What we collect

Only the nickname you type when you join a game. There are no accounts, and we do not ask for your email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.

What we store

Game moves and chat messages are passed between the players in a room and kept in the server's memory only while the room is active. They are deleted when the room closes. We do not keep chat history or a list of players.

Your settings and nickname are saved on your own device only.

Technical logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard technical connection logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operation. These do not include chat text.

Fair dice

Every dice roll is generated on the server by a secure random generator. There are no scripts and no fixed outcomes.

Chat rules

  • Be respectful. No insults, threats, hate or harassment.
  • Do not share private details such as phone numbers or addresses.
  • No sexual, illegal or offensive content.

Common abusive words are hidden automatically. You can mute any player by tapping their name above the board. Everyone must follow the laws that apply to them, including UAE law.

Children

The game is for general audiences. Younger children should play with a parent's or guardian's permission and supervision.

Report a problem

To report abuse or ask a privacy question, email noscriptludo@gmail.com. Please include the room code and the player's nickname. We will review reports and act where we can.

Playing

By playing you agree to these rules. The game is provided as is, and we may change these terms or stop the service at any time.
